The visual language for the app: Tang mineral pigments on 宣紙 paper, or ink-black lacquer with gold. This page is the approval artifact for T20. Once approved, T21 lifts the tokens and component states into code. Every sample uses Li Bai's 《靜夜思》, the prototype poem.

色 Pigment tokens

Seven Tang pigments and a five-step surface ramp, each with a light and a dark value. Both panels render at once, whatever the page theme. Pigments are lifted in dark mode so they hold contrast on lacquer. Gold takes over the heading color.

注 Ruby: bopomofo right, pinyin top

Bopomofo stacks in a column to the right of each character, with the tone mark beside it (˙ sits above), as in Taiwan school texts. Pinyin sits above, in italic. Romanization follows the audio language and can be turned off. Browsers barely support ruby-position: inter-character, so RubyChar lays out bopomofo with an inline grid, as shown here.

識 CharTile: lexicon states

These states are shared across poems. New gets a 石青 wash with an underline. Learning 1–4 is 藤黃 that fades one step per level. Known and Ignored get no highlight, and Ignored is also dimmed. The underline and the level pips mean the state still reads without color.

篇 Tangle tiles

Four feedback states, each paired with a glyph in the corner so the board stays colorblind-safe. Bank tiles take the best color revealed so far. Tiles already used in a solved line fade to 28%.

印 Seals

The six ranks are 白文 seals: paper-colored characters cut out of 朱砂, with a hand-cut, slightly irregular edge. 狀元 becomes the gold "已背 Memorized" seal. A locked rank is an empty outline. When a review is overdue, the seal fades and loses saturation until you review.

霧 Fading scroll

Fade is set per character, from that character's mastery. The scroll goes from full support to blank 田字格 paper. The levels below show one line at each step. In a real session, characters in the same line can be at different levels.

動 Motion

Three moments, each under 0.8 s, all switched off by prefers-reduced-motion. The scroll unrolls from the center when a poem opens. Ink bleeds outward on a correct answer. A seal stamps with an overshoot on rank-up.

定 Decisions to approve

Approving this PR signs off on the choices below. Comment on any you want changed.

  1. Palette values for both themes (section 1). Dark mode lifts each pigment's lightness and swaps the heading color to gold.
  2. Feedback mapping: 石綠 〇 right spot · 藤黃 △ in this line · 紫 ◇ another line · 墨 ✕ not in the poem. Share grid emoji: 🟩 🟨 🟪 ⬛.
  3. Bopomofo layout: a custom inline grid rather than native inter-character ruby, because Chrome and Safari don't support it.
  4. Lexicon states: New is a blue wash with an underline. Learning is four fading yellow steps with pips. Known and Ignored get no highlight.
  5. Fade ladder: full → no readings → mist → first character only (首字) → blank 田字格.
  6. Layout: horizontal poem text by default. The vertical right-to-left layout is shown on the Session landing and stays an option.
  7. Continue is the only primary action in a poem. Reading mode (賞) is a secondary entry from the Library card and the Session header.
  8. Fonts load from Google Fonts at runtime. T21 may self-host subsets to work offline in the PWA.
